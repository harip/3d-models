Designed for diorama displays, gondola/cable car alpine landscapes, tabletop terrain, or holiday scenery.

## 🖨️ FDM 3D Printing Recommendations

1. **Print Orientation**: All models sit 100% flat on the build plate at Z = 0.
2. **Supports**: **0% SUPPORTS NEEDED!** All overhangs incorporate self-supporting 38°–45° angles.
3. **Layer Height**: 0.12 mm – 0.16 mm for sharp, crisp low-poly rock facet detailing.
4. **Infill**: 15% – 20% (Gyroid or Grid).
5. **Nozzle Size**: 0.4 mm standard.
